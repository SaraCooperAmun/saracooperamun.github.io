### Overview

Haru is a tabletop social robot developed at Honda Research Institute Japan, designed to study and enable socio-emotional interaction between humans and robots. The platform focuses on expressive communication through gaze, motion, and sound, allowing the robot to engage users in intuitive and socially meaningful ways. Rather than performing task-oriented actions, Haru is designed to explore how robots can communicate affect, attention, and engagement in everyday interactions.

Over the course of a year at Honda Research Institute Japan, I worked on the development of socio-emotional interaction mechanisms for Haru, contributing to research on expressive behaviours, interaction design, and human–robot engagement. This work explored how multimodal cues such as eye gaze, timing, and movement can be combined to create more natural and engaging interactions.

### System Description

Haru is designed as an expressive robotic system capable of conveying internal states and intentions through coordinated multimodal behaviours. The robot integrates visual perception, behaviour generation, and real-time interaction control to support dynamic engagement with users.

The interaction system combines:

- Expressive head and eye movements to direct attention and convey intent  
- Sound and timing-based behaviours to reinforce emotional expression  
- Behaviour coordination enabling turn-taking and responsive interaction  
- Perception modules allowing the robot to detect and respond to users in real time  

These components are integrated into a behaviour-based architecture that enables fluid transitions between actions, allowing Haru to maintain engagement over extended interactions. The system is designed to prioritise responsiveness and legibility of behaviour, supporting research into how users interpret and respond to robot social signals.

### Demonstration

Haru was presented at the AI For Good Global Summit 2023 in Geneva, where it was demonstrated as an interactive social robot engaging directly with visitors. The demonstration focused on real-time interaction, allowing participants to experience different aspects of Haru’s expressive capabilities:

- Face and gaze behaviours enabling attention and engagement  
- Interactive storytelling where users co-create narratives with the robot  
- Photo capture and printing as part of a playful social interaction  

The AI For Good Global Summit is an international event organised by the United Nations, bringing together researchers, industry, and policymakers to explore the use of artificial intelligence for societal impact.
