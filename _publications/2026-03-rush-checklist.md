A practical framework for improving the clarity, transparency, and replicability of user studies in human‑robot interaction research. The RUSH checklist provides authors and reviewers with a structured set of reporting items, analogous to PRISMA or CONSORT in other fields, tailored to the needs of HRI research and evaluation.
