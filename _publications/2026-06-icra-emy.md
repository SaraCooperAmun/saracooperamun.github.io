This workshop paper introduces a unified multimodal communication architecture for a compact social robot used in autism therapy. The system combines speech, touch, gestures, pointing, and gaze cues within a single perception framework, and coordinates expressive behaviours through a centralized Interaction Manager. Multimodal inputs are normalized into unified intents, while expressive outputs such as emotional TTS, LEDs, facial expressions, head motion, gaze, and vocalizations are synchronized through a single interface. The architecture enables coherent and predictable behaviours that support therapeutic activities requiring coordinated multimodal interaction.
